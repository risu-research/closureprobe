# Claim Boundary

## Supported claim

Given a normalized trace of observable stages and pinned source profiles,
ClosureProbe deterministically tests whether each asserted negative is licensed
by the exact request, validated traversal evidence, scope, and bound proposition.
It localizes the earliest observed boundary where closure guards are lost,
mutated, mismatched, or replaced by unverified evidence.

## Novel contribution of the artifact

ClosureProbe combines, in one bounded executable system:

- producer-specific definitions of a valid root or aggregate evidence unit;
- explicit separation of a final page from a complete traversal;
- exact-request and exact-negative-proposition binding;
- a deterministic multi-axis negative-license oracle;
- receiver-side reconstruction of claimed new evidence; and
- cross-boundary localization from source through model or agent claim.

The contribution is the composition and executable falsification boundary. The
repository does not claim to invent pagination, query completeness, closed-world
reasoning, abstention, provenance, authenticated denial of existence, or the
general phenomenon of semantic laundering.

## Falsifiable result

For a named trace and pinned artifacts, ClosureProbe either derives the frozen
assessment and findings or it does not. All headline counts are regenerated by
the published CLI. The rc2 evidence is 45/45 only for its published corpus; it
is not a measured error rate for the wider world.
