# Claim Boundary

## Supported claim

Given supplied canonical request/response evidence, declared machine-readable
source grounding, a normalized observable trace, and pinned profiles,
ClosureProbe deterministically tests whether each asserted negative retains a
receiver-anchored, exact-query, context-, scope-, traversal-, and proposition-
bound license. It localizes the earliest observed boundary where that chain is
lost, mutated, substituted, or strengthened without reconstructed evidence.

## Novel contribution of the artifact

ClosureProbe combines, in one bounded executable system:

- producer-specific definitions of a valid root or aggregate evidence unit;
- mandatory receiver reconstruction of the trace root;
- explicit separation of final page from complete traversal;
- independent bindings for request, traversal, source grounding, and negative
  proposition;
- an `evidenceAnchored` state propagated across transformations;
- receiver reconstruction of legitimate new evidence; and
- first-boundary localization from source through model or agent claim.

The contribution is the composition and executable falsification boundary. The
repository does not claim to invent pagination, query completeness,
completeness-sensitive negative reasoning, closed-world reasoning, abstention,
provenance, semantic laundering, or authenticated denial of existence.

## Falsifiable result

For a named trace and pinned artifacts, ClosureProbe either derives the frozen
assessment and findings or it does not. The rc3 evidence is 50/50 for its
published corpus; it is not a measured error rate for external clients or the
wider world.
